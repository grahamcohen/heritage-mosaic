Heritage Mosaic

Help and support

Heritage Mosaic keeps the stories your family tells in one private place. Record a video or your voice, write it down, or share a photo and the story behind it.

Need a hand? Email mindflygame@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

Getting started

How do I invite my family?

Open the Family tab and tap Share invite, or copy your family's code (it looks like ABCD-234). Relatives download Heritage Mosaic, create an account, choose Join with a code, and enter it.

Can I belong to more than one family?

Yes. You can join as many families as you like with their codes. On the free plan you can start one family yourself, and Plus lets you start up to three. Switch between families with the arrows at the top of the Home and Family tabs.

Who can see what I share?

Only the members of the family you share it with. Nothing is public, and there's no feed of strangers.

Stories

What kinds of stories can I tell?

Video, voice, writing, or a photo with its story. Pick one of nearly 100 questions, answer a question someone in your family asked you, or ask your own.

Can I edit or delete a story?

Open the story and use the menu in the top corner. You can rename stories you shared and delete them. Whoever started the family can also remove stories if needed.

How do transcripts work?

With Plus, open a video or voice story and tap Create a transcript. Your iPhone writes out what was said, and everyone in the family can read and search it.

Plans and billing

What does Plus include?

As many video and voice stories as you like, recordings up to 30 minutes, transcripts, a printable story book, no member limit, and 20 GB of space per family. One subscription covers the whole family and up to three families.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Heritage Mosaic. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Plus but don't see it.

In the app, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

Your account

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? and we'll email you a link to choose a new one.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your profile, every story you shared, and your comments. Stories other family members shared stay with the family.
